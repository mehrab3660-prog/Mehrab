# 3D asset sources (Sprint 9 redesign)

All models below are CC0 (public domain) — free for personal and commercial
use, no attribution required. Credited here anyway for provenance.

## Exterior (`public/models/exterior/`)

Source: [KenneyNL/Starter-Kit-City-Builder](https://github.com/KenneyNL/Starter-Kit-City-Builder)
(official Kenney GitHub org repository). README: "Assets included in this
package (2D sprites, 3D models and sound effects) are CC0 licensed."
Original author: [Kenney](https://kenney.nl).

- `building-small-b.glb` — house exterior (materials re-tinted at runtime
  toward muted, natural tones — see `toneDownMaterial` in `Hero3DScene.tsx`;
  same geometry, calmer architectural color palette than the source's
  cartoon city-builder default)
- `grass-trees.glb`, `grass-trees-tall.glb` — trees

## Interior furniture (`public/models/interior/`)

Source: [RetroDECK/RetroQUEST](https://github.com/RetroDECK/RetroQUEST),
`assets/kenney_furniture-kit/` — a glTF conversion of Kenney's Furniture
Kit 2.0. That folder's own `License.txt` states: "This content is free to
use in personal, educational and commercial projects" (CC0), crediting
Kenney appreciated but not required. Original author: [Kenney](https://kenney.nl).

- `loungeDesignSofa.glb` — sofa
- `tableCoffee.glb` — coffee table
- `cabinetTelevision.glb` — TV unit
- `chairModernCushion.glb` — accent chair
- `pottedPlant.glb`, `rugRounded.glb` — decor
- `lampRoundFloor.glb` — floor lamp

No AI-generated, purchased, or proprietary assets were used. No model was
fabricated to represent a specific real Sultan Noor product — all
product-linked hotspots still resolve strictly from the live catalog API.
